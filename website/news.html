Announcements

Latest News

September 2026

New capstone stream for Software Development students

Diploma in Software Development students now choose a capstone track — web, mobile or data — in their final semester, building a portfolio project in that specialisation.

August 2026

91% graduate employment rate recorded for 2025 cohort

Our latest graduate survey shows 91% of 2025 graduates were employed, studying further or running their own business within six months of graduating.

July 2026

Springs Campus workshop upgrade completed

New machining, welding and fabrication equipment is now in use by Engineering Trades students, funded in part by our industry placement partners.

June 2026

Placement partnerships expanded for 2027 intake

We've added new partner firms across accounting, manufacturing and healthcare, growing semester-long placement opportunities for 2027 students.

May 2026

NSFAS guidance sessions now every Saturday

The admissions office now runs weekly Saturday sessions to help NSFAS-eligible applicants complete their funding applications alongside their RTC application.

April 2026

Community Health Work certificate adds evening classes

The Certificate in Community Health Work is now also offered in the evening, so students already working or volunteering in their communities can still qualify.
