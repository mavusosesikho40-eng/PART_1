About Rand Training College

Three decades of practical, career-focused education for students across Gauteng and beyond.

Our story

Founded on a simple idea

Rand Training College was established in 1994 by a group of educators and industry professionals who believed vocational education should be taken as seriously as any university degree. Starting with a single evening bookkeeping class of twelve students, the college has grown into a multi-faculty institution serving thousands of learners each year.

Today we offer 12 accredited certificate and diploma programmes across business, technology, engineering and health sciences, delivered from our Braamfontein and Springs campuses, with a growing number of courses available online.

What hasn't changed is our focus: every programme is built around what employers actually need, taught by people who have done the job.

At a glance

  • Founded 1994
  • Campuses 2, plus online
  • Accreditation DHET & QCTO registered
  • Alumni network 6,200+
Our mission

Preparing every student for a real career

To deliver accessible, high-quality vocational education that equips students with the practical skills, confidence and industry connections they need to build a lasting career.

Our vision

The region's most trusted training partner

To be recognised by employers and students alike as the first choice for practical, industry-aligned training in South Africa.
