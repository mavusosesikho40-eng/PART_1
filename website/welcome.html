A Welcome From Our Principal

A personal note from Dr. Nomvula Mahlangu on what RTC stands for, and what you can expect here.

From the Principal's desk

Welcome to Rand Training College

When I joined Rand Training College in 2015, I did so because I believed — and still believe — that where you study should matter less than whether you walk out able to do the job. That's the standard every one of our eighteen programmes is built to, and it's the same standard I hold myself to as Principal.

Whoever you are reading this — a Matric learner weighing up options, someone returning to study while working full-time, or a parent doing the research on their child's behalf — I want you to know what to expect here. Small classes, taught by people who have actually done the work they're teaching. Workshops and placements from early in your programme, not just in your final year. Fees and payment plans explained plainly, with no fine print designed to catch you out.

We are not the biggest college in Johannesburg, and we don't want to be. We want to be the one that gets you hired. Ninety-one percent of our graduates are in work, further study, or running their own business within six months of finishing — that number is the one I check every year, and the one I'm proudest of.

If you're considering applying, I'd encourage you to do it. If you still have questions first, our admissions team would rather spend twenty minutes answering them now than have you find out the answers were wrong after you've enrolled. Either way, welcome — I hope to see you on campus.

Dr. Nomvula Mahlangu Principal & CEO, Rand Training College
