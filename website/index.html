What we offer

Four faculties, one goal: work-ready graduates

Every programme combines classroom theory with hands-on practice, in small classes taught by instructors with real industry experience.

B

Business & Commerce

Accounting, office administration, marketing and entrepreneurship programmes built around real business practice.

T

Information Technology

Networking, software development and IT support training on current industry tools and platforms.

E

Engineering Trades

Electrical, mechanical and civil trade programmes with workshop hours from week one.

H

Health Sciences

Nursing auxiliary, primary health care and community health worker training with clinical placements.

Getting started

Four steps from enquiry to enrolment

  1. Choose a programme

    Browse our faculties and shortlist a certificate or diploma that matches your goals.

  2. Submit your application

    Complete the online application form with your details and supporting documents.

  3. Meet admissions requirements

    Our admissions office reviews your application and confirms you meet the entry requirements.

  4. Register and begin classes
