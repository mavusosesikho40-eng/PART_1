Admissions

Everything you need to know about applying to Rand Training College, from requirements to fees to key dates.

General requirements

What you need to apply

  • Grade 11 or Grade 12 (Matric) certificate, or equivalent NQF Level 4 qualification
  • Certified copy of your South African ID or passport
  • Proof of residence not older than three months
  • Completed application form and non-refundable application fee

Some diploma programmes (Financial Accounting, Software Development, Mechanical Engineering, Marketing & Entrepreneurship) require a Matric pass with Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy. Health Sciences programmes require a medical fitness declaration.

Fees & payment

Tuition & payment plans

  • Application fee: R 250 (non-refundable)
  • Registration fee: R 1,500, due on acceptance
  • Certificate programmes: from R 18,500 per year
  • Diploma programmes: from R 26,900 per year

Interest-free monthly payment plans are available for all programmes. NSFAS-eligible students should contact the admissions office for guidance on funding applications.
