Diploma in Mechanical Engineering

Machining, fabrication and maintenance training with a semester of workplace experience.

+ Add a photo at images/programs/mechanical-engineering.jpg
Overview

About this programme

Students train on real machine tools and fabrication equipment from the first semester, building up from manual machining to maintenance planning and basic mechanical design.

A semester-long workplace placement with a partner manufacturer or engineering firm gives students supervised, hands-on experience before graduation.

What you'll learn

  • Manual and CNC machining
  • Welding and fabrication
  • Mechanical drawing and design basics
  • Maintenance planning and reliability
  • Workplace health and safety

Career outcomes

Graduates move into mechanical technician, maintenance and junior engineering support roles in manufacturing and industrial settings.

  • Mechanical Technician
  • Maintenance Technician
  • Junior Engineering Assistant

Quick Facts

  • Duration 2 Years
  • Study Mode Full-time
  • Qualification NQF Level 6
  • Faculty Engineering Trades
  • Campus Springs Campus

Matric (or equivalent) with Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy.
