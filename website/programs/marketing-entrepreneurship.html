Diploma in Marketing & Entrepreneurship

From market research to digital campaigns, finishing with a capstone business plan students can take to market.

+ Add a photo at images/programs/marketing-entrepreneurship.jpg
Overview

About this programme

This diploma covers both sides of growing a business: understanding and reaching customers through marketing, and the practical steps of starting and running a venture. Students work on real brands and mock campaigns throughout the programme.

The programme builds toward a capstone project: a complete business plan and marketing strategy that students pitch in their final semester, ready to take to funders or into their own venture.

What you'll learn

  • Market research and consumer behaviour
  • Digital marketing and social media campaigns
  • Branding and advertising fundamentals
  • Small business finance and planning
  • Sales strategy and pitching

Career outcomes

Graduates move into marketing coordinator and sales roles, or launch their own small businesses using the plan developed on the programme.

  • Marketing Coordinator
  • Social Media Coordinator
  • Small Business Owner

Quick Facts

  • Duration 2 Years
