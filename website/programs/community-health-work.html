Certificate in Community Health Work

Trains students to support households and clinics as frontline community health workers.

+ Add a photo at images/programs/community-health-work.jpg
Overview

About this programme

Community health workers are often the first point of contact between a household and the health system. This certificate trains students to support families directly, in their homes and in local clinics.

Evening classes are available so students already working or volunteering in their communities can qualify without stopping that work.

What you'll learn

  • Household health screening
  • Chronic disease support (e.g. TB, HIV adherence)
  • Health education for families
  • Working with clinics and referral systems
  • Basic record-keeping and reporting

Career outcomes

Graduates are employed by clinics, NGOs and community health programmes as frontline community health workers.

  • Community Health Worker
  • Home-Based Care Worker
  • Health Promoter

Quick Facts

  • Duration 9 Months
  • Study Mode Full-time or Evening
  • Qualification NQF Level 4
  • Faculty Health Sciences
  • Campus Braamfontein Campus

Grade 11 (or equivalent) and a medical fitness declaration.
