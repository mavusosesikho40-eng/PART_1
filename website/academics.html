Academics & Programmes

18 accredited certificate and diploma programmes across four faculties, each built around what employers are hiring for.

Faculty of Business & Commerce

Business & Commerce

Grounded in real bookkeeping, office and marketing practice — not just theory.

Diploma in Financial Accounting

2 years Full-time NQF Level 6

Covers bookkeeping, taxation, payroll and financial reporting, with a semester-long practicum at a partner firm.

Certificate in Office Administration

1 year Full-time or evening NQF Level 5

Business communication, records management, and the software skills every modern office runs on.

Diploma in Marketing & Entrepreneurship

2 years Full-time NQF Level 6

From market research to digital campaigns, finishing with a capstone business plan students can take to market.

Faculty of Information Technology

Information Technology

Hands-on labs from week one, on the same tools used in industry.

Diploma in Software Development

2 years Full-time NQF Level 6

Programming fundamentals through to full-stack web development, with a portfolio project for graduation.

Certificate in Network Administration

1 year Full-time NQF Level 5

Prepares students for industry networking certifications alongside practical lab work.

Certificate in IT Support & Technical Helpdesk

1 year Full-time or evening NQF Level 5

Troubleshooting, hardware and customer-facing support skills for entry-level IT careers.

Faculty of Engineering Trades

Engineering Trades

Workshop-based training toward trade test readiness.

Certificate in Electrical Installation

18 months Full-time NQF Level 4

Wiring, safety regulations and installation practice, preparing students for trade testing.

Diploma in Mechanical Engineering

2 years Full-time NQF Level 6

Machining, fabrication and maintenance training with a semester of workplace experience.

Certificate in Civil Engineering Technology

18 months Full-time NQF Level 4

Site surveying, materials and construction fundamentals for entry into the built environment sector.

Faculty of Health Sciences

Health Sciences

Clinical placements built into every programme, from year one.

Certificate in Nursing Auxiliary Studies

1 year Full-time NQF Level 5

Patient care fundamentals with supervised clinical placement hours at partner facilities.

Certificate in Primary Health Care

1 year Full-time NQF Level 5

Community-based health promotion, first-line treatment and referral practice.

Certificate in Community Health Work

9 months Full-time or evening NQF Level 4

Trains students to support households and clinics as frontline community health workers.
